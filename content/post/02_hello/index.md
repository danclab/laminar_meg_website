## A little bit of history 

One day, in the UCL lab, Sven Bestmann, Luzia Troebinger and Gareth Barnes had this crazy idea that maybe we could push the spatial resolution of MEG, known to be already quite good to distinguish between superficial and deep activity. For this, they knew it: we needed to know very well the position of the sensors relative to the head. 
For this was born the "headcast" a specific and not very confortable at first molded
