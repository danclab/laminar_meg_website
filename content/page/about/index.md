## Who is this for?

Laminar MEG is an open forum for discussing emerging methods, preliminary results, unresolved problems, and validation strategies for relating MEG signals to cortical depth. The scope includes conventional MEG and OPM, source reconstruction, simulations, biophysical modelling, and invasive or cross-modal work where it directly informs laminar MEG.
