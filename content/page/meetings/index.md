LaminarMEG meets regularly to discuss methods, preliminary results, and open questions about relating MEG signals to cortical depth. When speakers agree to share their sessions, the recordings will appear here.

For future meetings, see the [calendar](/calendar/). To join the discussion, [subscribe to the mailing list](https://listes.services.cnrs.fr/wws/subscribe/laminarmeg).

## Past meetings

### 13 November 2026 - Solène Gailhard

**Meeting #1 · EBBlayer and laminar source reconstruction**

In our first meeting, Solène Gailhard (DANClab, CNRS, Lyon) presented her ongoing work on EBBlayer and methods for improving laminar MEG source reconstruction, followed by a community discussion.
